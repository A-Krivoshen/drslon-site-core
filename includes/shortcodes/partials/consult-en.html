Common questions

How does a consultation work?

You pick the channel: Yandex Telemost (better for video), Telegram, WhatsApp or text. We agree what the task is. At the set time we connect. A recording only if you or I agree to it.

What is included?

A look at the task, advice, steps, and answers to your questions. Afterwards you get a checklist, links, source files if there were any, a recording if you wanted one, and what to do next.

Can it stay anonymous?

Yes. We can stay on the technical part. Contact details and the correspondence are not passed to anyone else.

Which topics?

WordPress, hosting, domains, VPS, Linux, Nginx, Docker, security, speed, mail, integrations, automation, and finding where a technical problem actually is. If you are not sure, write anyway.

How do I pay?

After the consultation you get a payment link. Robokassa, YooMoney, a card or a transfer. It is on the offer. A receipt if you need one.

Is there a refund?

If the consultation does not happen because of me, the money comes back. If the task is outside what I do, I do not keep the payment.
